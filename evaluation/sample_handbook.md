
# Northstar Learning Services
## Employee Handbook

This handbook describes the internal policies of Northstar Learning Services, a fictional company created for testing a document question-answering application. It is the source of truth for the rules described below.

## 1. Working Hours

The normal working week is Monday to Friday. Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Employees may choose a flexible start time between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM with their manager's approval, provided they complete eight working hours. The lunch break is not counted as working time.

Employees working remotely must remain available during agreed team hours, update their work status, and attend scheduled meetings. Remote work is permitted for up to two days each week with manager approval.

## 2. Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave and 8 days of sick leave each calendar year. A maximum of five unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next year. Any carried-over leave expires on March 31.

Employees requesting one or two days of leave should submit the request at least three business days in advance. Requests for more than two consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Managers should respond to leave requests within two working days. Sick employees should notify their manager as early as possible.

## 3. IT Equipment

The company provides employees with a laptop and the equipment required for their role. Company devices must be used in accordance with IT policies. Employees must not install unapproved software or share their work accounts with other people.

Employees must lock their screens whenever they leave their desks. A lost or stolen device must be reported to both the IT team and the employee's manager within one hour of discovery. Company equipment must be returned when employment ends.

## 4. Information Security

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for company accounts. Passwords must never be shared. Employees who receive a suspicious email should report it to security@northstar.example within 30 minutes of noticing it.

Employees working on public Wi-Fi must use the company VPN. Customer information may only be stored in company-approved drives. Raw customer files must be deleted no later than 90 days after the relevant project ends, unless a longer retention period is formally approved.

## 5. Expenses and Travel

Employees must submit expense claims within 10 calendar days of the expense date. A receipt is required for every individual expense above ₹500. Claims should include the business purpose, date, amount, and project reference.

Approved business rail travel is reimbursed at economy class or the standard available class. Taxi expenses require prior manager approval. Personal purchases and unapproved upgrades are not reimbursable.

## 6. Customer Support

Customer requests must be recorded in the company's customer relationship management (CRM) system. Employees must not promise a delivery date, refund, or product feature before obtaining the necessary internal approval.

Urgent support tickets should receive an initial response within one hour. High-priority tickets should receive an initial response within four business hours. Normal-priority tickets should receive an initial response within one business day. These targets refer to the first response, not necessarily final resolution.

## 7. Training and Development

New employees must complete onboarding within their first five working days. Information-security and compliance training must be completed annually. Employees may use four hours per quarter for approved professional development activities.

Training requests should be discussed with the employee's manager before registration. Employees should record completed training in the company's learning tracker.

## 8. Meetings and Communication

Employees should review meeting invitations and accept or decline them promptly. Meeting organizers should share an agenda for meetings expected to last more than 30 minutes. Decisions and action items should be recorded and shared with participants.

Internal business communication should use approved company accounts. Confidential information must not be posted on public social media or sent to personal email accounts.
